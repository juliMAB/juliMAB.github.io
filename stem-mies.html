The Premise

Imagine a world where music is corrupted, and only you can restore it with rhythm. Welcome to Stemmies, a 3D rhythm-adventure experience that fuses the precision of a rhythm game with the exploration and narrative of a platformer.

You are a Guardian, sent to the world of Sonos to purify the corruption silencing its melodies. Accompanied by Daw, a robot with a TV screen for a face, you will journey through surreal landscapes—from pulsating clouds to dystopian cities—where each level is a song and every action is a note.

Core Gameplay: Rhythm is Action

Every move is synced to the soundtrack. You don't just hit notes on a lane; you jump, deflect, and absorb to the beat of the music to overcome obstacles and defeat the Stemmies, the manifestations of corruption.

Executing perfect actions builds a combo that not only boosts your score but literally adds new instrumental layers to the song and triggers spectacular environmental animations, making you feel in total sync with the world.

Key Features

Boss Encounters: Face epic battles against Boss Stemmies, where the soundtrack shifts entirely, forcing you to dodge beams, deflect projectiles, and absorb energy in a visual and auditory spectacle.

Traccs: Power up your play with "virtue badges" that charge through rhythmic actions and allow unique abilities like revealing hidden coins or easing hazards.

GameFace: Show off your skills! This mode randomly activates, recording your screen and reaction as you attempt perfect "On Beat" hits. Share your epic moments directly to social media.

Immersive Narrative: Uncover the story of Sonos by collecting Glyphs, ancient memory imprints that reveal the world's lore and unlock permanent upgrades.

Music-Integrated Economy

As a platform for both emerging and established artists, Stemmies allows players to discover new music and, with one click, add the game's songs to their Spotify or Apple Music libraries, generating royalties for the creators.

Stemmies is more than a game; it's a synergy between player, music, and world. It's the thrill of a drum solo, the tension of a perfectly timed jump, and the satisfaction of watching the world come alive to your rhythm.

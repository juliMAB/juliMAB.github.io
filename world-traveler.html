Play the Game

Try out World Traveler directly in your browser! Tap or click to change the plane's direction.

Note: Game optimized for mobile devices. Use touch controls or mouse clicks to play.

About World Traveler

This project was born from the capital generated by our first game, which allowed us to finance World Traveler to demonstrate our expertise in developing Android projects. Currently, the project is uploaded to Google Play Console as an internal release, and our goal is to publish it to showcase our skills in creating hypercasual games that are simple, visually appealing, and engaging.

Gameplay Mechanics

The core gameplay is elegantly simple: your plane flies forward automatically, and you tap the screen to change its direction. This creates an engaging experience that's easy to learn but challenging to master as you navigate through increasingly complex environments.

Key Features

Simple one-touch controls - tap to change direction • Beautiful voxel art style with vibrant environments • Shop system with various plane skins and customization • Global leaderboard to compete with other players • Achievement system with multiple challenges • Hypercasual gameplay perfect for quick sessions • Smooth performance optimized for Android devices

Voxel Art Design

The game features a distinctive voxel art style that creates a charming, blocky aesthetic reminiscent of modern indie games. Each environment is carefully crafted with attention to color harmony and visual appeal, making the flying experience both relaxing and visually engaging.
